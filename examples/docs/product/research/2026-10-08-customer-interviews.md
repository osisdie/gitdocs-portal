# Customer interviews — October

Twelve interviews (8 Team, 4 Enterprise). Personas: operations lead, developer, finance admin.

## Findings

1. **Onboarding is slow.** 7 of 12 needed help connecting their first integration. The empty dashboard gives no next
   step. → onboarding UX mockups.
2. **Search is the missing feature.** Operations leads want to search orders by customer, SKU and date.
   → search tools comparison.
3. **Pricing is unclear.** Finance admins could not predict the bill when API usage spikes. → pricing experiment.
4. **SSO is a must for Enterprise.** Three of four Enterprise customers asked for SSO with their identity provider.

## Quotes

> "I want to see where my order is without calling support." — operations lead

> "Give me a budget alert before the bill surprises me." — finance admin
