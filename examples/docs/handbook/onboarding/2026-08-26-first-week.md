# Your first week at Northwind

Welcome aboard! This onboarding checklist covers the first five days. Your buddy and your manager are listed in the
welcome mail.

## Day 1 — accounts

- Sign in once with SSO; every internal tool uses the same identity provider.
- Enrol MFA on your phone before lunch: the security policy blocks accounts without it after 24 hours.
- Read the [access and SSO policy](../security/2026-09-10-access-policy.md).

## Day 2 — laptop and tools

Follow the developer environment guide. Most new hires are productive by the afternoon.

## Day 3–4 — shadowing

Join the on-call shadow rotation for one shift and sit in a customer interview with the research team.
Onboarding feedback from these sessions feeds the onboarding UX work in product research.

## Day 5 — first change

Ship something small: a typo fix in the docs portal counts. The pipeline runs tests and deploys a preview.
